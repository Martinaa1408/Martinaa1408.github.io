Program overview

The Bachelor’s program in Biotechnology at the University of Bologna provides an interdisciplinary background across biological systems and applied scientific methodologies. A distinctive component of the program is its strong focus on hands-on laboratory training, including single-bench lab practicals and a mandatory experimental internship.

BSc Thesis & Curricular Internship (Biotechnology)

University of Bologna · 2024

Thesis topic

My bachelor thesis investigated synthetic torpor in a non-hibernating model (rat), focusing on the histopathological profile and gene expression of skeletal muscle after pharmacological inhibition of the thermoregulatory nucleus Raphe pallidus (RPa).

The study combined histology (H&E staining and microscopy) with RNA-seq analysis to identify differentially expressed genes (DEGs) between hypothermic and normothermic groups, highlighting pathways related to lipid metabolism, muscle preservation and immune response modulation.

Curricular internship

The curricular internship was carried out under the supervision of Prof. Roberto Amici, within the Department of Physiology (San Donato campus), for approximately 3 months (March–May 2024). The work was part of the research group on autonomic nervous system physiology and behavior.
